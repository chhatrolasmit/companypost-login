Company Post

Company Post is a phone app for the account owner. A post is prepared on the phone. It goes to Instagram only after the owner says yes.

The owner logs in on Meta's own screen. The app does not ask for the Instagram password. The connection stays on that phone.

A picture or reel is placed on a public link so Instagram can fetch it.

To remove a company, open the app and remove that company. For a deletion request, email vavetra.in@gmail.com.
